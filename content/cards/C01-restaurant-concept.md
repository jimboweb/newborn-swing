## Goal
Name your restaurant and decide what food it serves.

## What to decide

- **Name** — What is your restaurant called?
- **Cuisine** — What kind of food does it serve? (Italian, tacos, sushi, burgers, breakfast, ...)
- **Vibe** — Is it fancy? Casual? Fast? Family-style? Cozy? Loud and fun?
- **One sentence** — Write a single sentence that describes your restaurant as if you were telling a friend about it.

## Why this comes first

You are about to build a website for this restaurant. Every decision from here on — what words go on the page, what pictures you use, what colors you pick — flows from this idea. Get it clear in your head now before you touch any code.

## Self-check

You can answer all of these without looking at notes:

- What is the name of my restaurant?
- What kind of food does it serve?
- Who would eat there?
- What is the one-sentence description?

When you can answer all four, you are ready for **C02**.
