## Goal
Read and understand the standard your finished website must meet.

## What is a gate checkpoint?

C03 is a **gate**. Before you start building, you read the criteria below, we discuss any questions, and you agree to be held to them. This is how professional software teams work — everyone agrees on what "done" means *before* the code is written, not after.

The visiting elementary school students will use your finished site. These criteria protect them.

## The acceptance criteria

**Content**
- [ ] The page title (shown in the browser tab) includes your restaurant's name
- [ ] There is a main heading with the restaurant name visible on the page
- [ ] The menu has at least four items, each with a name, a short description, and a price
- [ ] The restaurant's hours are shown
- [ ] There is an "About" section with at least two sentences about the restaurant
- [ ] At least one image appears on the page

**Code**
- [ ] Presentation lives in a separate CSS file, linked with a `<link>` tag — no `style=""` attributes on HTML elements
- [ ] The page passes the W3C HTML validator with no errors
- [ ] Every image has a descriptive `alt` attribute

**Readability**
- [ ] Text and background colours have enough contrast that a younger student can read it comfortably
- [ ] The largest heading is at least 24px
- [ ] The page makes sense to someone who has never heard of your restaurant

## Self-check

Read every item above. If any of them surprises you or raises a question, ask now — before you build anything.

When you understand all of them, talk to Mr. Stewart. Bring your content model (C02). This conversation is the gate.
